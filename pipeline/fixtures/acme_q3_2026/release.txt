Acme Software Reports Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 Results

SAN JOSE, Calif., October 13, 2026. Acme Software, Inc. (NASDAQ: ACME) today announced results for its third fiscal quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Third quarter revenue was $412 million, up 18% year over year. Subscription revenue was $371 million, up 21% year over year. Non-GAAP operating margin was 23%. Free cash flow was $96 million. Net revenue retention was 114%. Customers with more than $100,000 in annual recurring revenue grew to 1,240.

GAAP net income was $31 million, or $0.19 per diluted share. Non-GAAP net income was $74 million, or $0.45 per diluted share.

"Q3 was a strong quarter across the board, with growth in every region and record enterprise bookings," said Dana Whitfield, CEO.

Financial Outlook
For the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, Acme expects revenue of $425 million to $429 million and non-GAAP operating margin of approximately 23%.
For the full fiscal year 2026, Acme reiterates its prior guidance of revenue of $1.61 billion to $1.62 billion.
