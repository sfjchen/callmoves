Bristol Myers Squibb Reports Third Quarter Financial Results for 2026 and Raises Full-Year Revenue Outlook
Growth Portfolio Performance and Disciplined Execution Drive Strong Financial Results
Key Pipeline Milestones Include Regulatory Approvals and Positive Registrational Data Across Multiple Therapeutic Areas

• Third quarter revenues increased 7% to $13.1 billion
◦ Growth Portfolio revenues increased 12% to $7.7 billion
• GAAP EPS was $1.05 and non-GAAP EPS was $1.35; Both figures include net impact of $(0.58) due to Acquired IPRD charges and licensing income
• Raising 2026 revenue guidance to a range of $49.75 billion to $50.25 billion; Updating non-GAAP EPS range to $6.35 to $6.55, inclusive of a $(0.60) per share net impact from Acquired IPRD charges and licensing income
(PRINCETON, N.J., October 29, 2026) – Bristol Myers Squibb (NYSE: BMY) today reports results for the third quarter of 2026.

“We delivered another quarter of double-digit growth in our Growth Portfolio, which increased 12% and reflects the continued momentum across our diversified portfolio," said Christopher Boerner, Ph.D. , board chair and chief executive officer, Bristol Myers Squibb. "The approval of Zenbexus , the first-in-class CELMoD, brings a new class of treatment to market for patients with multiple myeloma and is one of several recent pipeline milestones. As we look forward to additional late-stage data in the near term, we remain focused on executing with discipline, investing where we see the greatest opportunity and strengthening the foundation for sustainable growth."

PRODUCT AND PIPELINE UPDATES
Entries organized by date and inclusive of third quarter and recent updates.

Asset(s) Date Announced
Milestone
Izalontamab Brengitecan (iza-bren) October 26 Announced with SystImmune that SystImmune's parent company, Sichuan Biokin Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd., reported positive results from a prespecified interim analysis in the Phase 3 PANKU-Lung01 study evaluating iza-bren versus platinum-based chemotherapy (PBC) in patients with EGFR-mutated non-squamous non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) who were previously treated with an EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor. Results showed that iza-bren significantly improved median progression-free survival (PFS) compared to PBC.

pumitamig October 25 Announced with BioNTech SE positive data from the Phase 2 ROSETTA Lung-107 trial evaluating pumitamig in combination with docetaxel in patients with metastatic NSCLC whose disease progressed on or after first-line immunotherapy plus chemotherapy. Data showed encouraging anti-tumor activity, with responses observed in both squamous and non-squamous NSCLC and across PD-L1 expression levels, together with a manageable safety profile consistent with prior global datasets for pumitamig.

Zolacabtagene Autoleucel (zola-cel) October 23 Announced disclosure of interim safety and efficacy results from the multiple sclerosis (MS) cohorts of the Phase 1 Breakfree-2 study evaluating zola-cel in patients with highly active relapsing MS (haRMS) and progressive forms of MS (PMS). The data show a safety profile consistent with previously reported zola-cel Phase 1 clinical trial data and early, promising efficacy across both haRMS and PMS.

iza-bren October 23 Announced with SystImmune that SystImmune's parent company, Sichuan Biokin Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd., reported positive results from a prespecified interim analysis of the Phase 3 PANKU-Breast01 study evaluating iza-bren versus chemotherapy in patients with unresectable locally advanced, recurrent or metastatic hormone receptor-positive/HER2-negative breast cancer who have failed at least one prior line of chemotherapy. Results showed a 59% reduction in the risk of disease progression or death, with median PFS vs. chemotherapy of 8.2 months vs. 4.0 months. The safety profile for iza-bren was manageable and no new safety signals were identified.

RYZ101 October 12 Announced positive topline results from the interim analysis of the Phase 3 ACTION-1 trial evaluating RYZ101 versus investigator's choice standard of care in patients with somatostatin receptor-positive gastroenteropancreatic neuroendocrine tumors who have progressed following Lutetium-177-based therapy. The study met the primary endpoint with a statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvement in PFS by blinded independent central review, and safety results consistent with the known RYZ101 safety profile.

Admilparant October 12 Announced positive topline results from the Phase 3 ALOFT-IPF trial evaluating the efficacy, safety and tolerability of admilparant in adults with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis. The trial met its primary endpoint at the 60mg dose, with admilparant demonstrating a statistically significant improvement compared to placebo in absolute change in forced vital capacity (FVC) from baseline at Week 52. At the 120mg dose, admilparant slowed the decline of FVC but was not statistically significant. Admilparant demonstrated a well-tolerated safety profile across both doses.

Sotyktu ® (deucravacitinib)
September 17 Announced positive results from the Phase 3 POETYK PsA-2 study , including its open-label extension, showing durable efficacy and a consistent safety profile for Sotyktu in adults with psoriatic arthritis.

Arlocabtagene Autoleucel (arlo-cel) September 8 Announced positive Phase 2 results from the registrational QUINTESSENTIAL trial evaluating arlo-cel in adult patients with quadruple-class exposed relapsed and refractory multiple myeloma. The study met its primary endpoint of demonstrating a statistically significant and clinically meaningful overall response rate, and also met its key secondary endpoints.

Camzyos August 29 Results from the EXPLORER-LTE cohort of the MAVA-LTE study reinforced the long-term efficacy and safety of Camzyos for the treatment of symptomatic obstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy. These data add to the understanding that Camzyos' effects are sustained for up to five years with continuous, consistent treatment.

Zenbexus August 13 The FDA approved Zenbexus in combination with daratumumab and hyaluronidase-fihj and dexamethasone for the treatment of adult patients with multiple myeloma who have received at least one prior line of therapy, including a proteasome inhibitor and an immunomodulatory agent. The approval is based on results from the Phase 3 EXCALIBER-RRMM trial.

Our Strategy
At Bristol Myers Squibb, our goal is to build a company that is financially strong and delivers industry-leading, sustainable growth into the 2030s and beyond.
As we advance our multi-year strategy to position the company for long-term growth, we are guided by the following priorities:
• Focusing R&D on high-impact, transformational medicines to treat life-threatening diseases;
• Embedding rigorous operational execution across the organization to build momentum in our Growth Portfolio comprised primarily of medicines early in their lifecycles; and
• Maintaining disciplined capital allocation to drive sustainable cash flow generation, balance sheet strength and long-term shareholder returns.
Strengthening U.S. Presence and Capabilities for Next-Generation Medicines
5

The company recently announced multiple manufacturing expansions as a part of its standing commitment to invest in the U.S. across research and development, technology and domestic manufacturing.
In August 2026, the company announced plans to develop a new, state-of-the-art manufacturing campus at Generation Park in Houston, Texas, designed to accelerate the delivery of next-generation medicines to patients. The multi-modal campus will represent an approximately $2.3 billion investment, create nearly 500 skilled jobs, and is designed to grow in scale, capability and workforce for decades in the future.
Also in August 2026, RayzeBio, Inc., a Bristol Myers Squibb company, said it would invest more than $173 million to expand its radiopharmaceutical manufacturing operations by establishing a new facility in Whitestown, Indiana. Construction is expected to begin this year on the approximately 225,000-square-foot facility, which will complement RayzeBio's existing footprint in the state and will create up to 100 new jobs by the end of 2029.
Financial Guidance
Bristol Myers Squibb is increasing its full-year, non-GAAP revenue guidance from a range of approximately $49.0 billion to $50.0 billion to a range of approximately $49.75 billion to $50.25 billion. This update primarily reflects broad-based and continuing momentum across the portfolio.
Full-year other income/(expense) is now expected to be ~$650 million of expense, due to favorable net interest.
Non-GAAP EPS is being updated to an anticipated range of $6.35 - $6.55, which is inclusive of a $(0.60) impact from Acquired IPRD and licensing income.
