New York, October 13, 2026 – Citigroup Inc. today reported net income for the third 
quarter 2026 of $5.2 billion, or $2.85 per diluted share, on revenues of $23.8 billion. 
This compares to net income of $3.8 billion, or $1.86 per diluted share, on revenues 
of $22.1 billion for the third quarter 2025. 
 
As previously disclosed (3), third quarter results in the prior-year period included a 
notable item consisting of a goodwill impairment of $726 million ($714 million 
after-tax), recorded in operating expenses, related to Citi’s agreement to sell a 25% 
equity stake in Grupo Financiero Banamex, S.A. de C.V. (Banamex). 
 
Revenues increased 8% from the prior -year period , driven by growth i n Services, 
Wealth, Markets and Banking, partially offset by a decline in All Other. 
 
Net income was $5.2 billion, compared to $3.8 billion in the prior-year period on a 
reported basis, driven by higher revenues and lower expenses, partially offset by a 
higher provision for credit losse s. Excluding the notable item , net income in the 
prior-year period was $4.5 billion(3). 
 
Earnings per share of $2.85 increased from $1.86 per diluted share in the prior-year 
period on a reported basis, reflecting higher net income and a lower share count due 
to share repurchases. Excluding the notable item , earnings per share in the prior -
year period was $2.24(3). 
 
Percentage comparisons throughout this press release are calculated for the third 
quarter 2026 versus the third quarter 2025, unless otherwise specified.

Citigroup 
Citigroup revenues of $23.8 billion in the third quarter 2026 increased 8%, driven by growth in Services, Wealth, 
Markets and Banking, partially offset by a decline in All Other. Net interest income increased 14%, driven by growth

Citigroup operating expenses of $14.0 billion were down 2% on a reported basis, driven by the absence of the 
notable item as well as lower professional services expenses, partially offset by higher transactional and product

Foundation. Excluding the notable item in the prior-year period, expenses were up 3% (3). 
 
Citigroup provision for credit losses was $2.6 billion, reflecting $2.2 billion of net credit losses and a net reserve

were unchanged from the prior-year period. The provision in the prior-year period was $2.5 billion, reflecting $2.2 
billion of net credit losses and a net reserve build of $236 million, driven by portfolio growth and transfer risk, 
partially offset by changes in the macroeconomic outlook.

provision for credit losses . Excluding the notable item, net income in the prior -year period was $4.5 billion (3).

period, driven by growth in Services. Citigroup’s average deposits were approximately $ 1.5 trillion in the third

Services 
Services revenues of $6.6 billion were up 15%, driven by growth in Treasury and Trade Solutions and Securities 
Services. Net interest income increased 17%, driven by higher average deposit balances and deposit spreads. Non-
interest revenue increased 12%, driven by higher fees. 
 
Treasury and Trade Solutions revenues of $4.9 billion were up 16%, driven by an 18% increase in net interest 
income and a 12% increase in non -interest revenue . The increase in net interest income was driven by higher 
average deposit balances and deposit spreads. The increase in non-interest revenue was driven by higher fees and 
underlying drivers, including cross -border transaction value up 10%, commercial card spend volume up 10% and 
U.S. dollar clearing volume up 5%, as well as a more favorable impact from currency devaluation in Argentina . 
 
Securities Services revenues of $1.7 billion were up 13%, driven by a 13% increase in non -interest revenue and a 
14% increase in net interest income. The increase in non -interest revenue was driven by higher fees, which 
benefited from a 16% increase in assets under custody and administration, which includes new assets onboarded 
as well as the impact of market valuations, partially offset by a mark-to-market gain in the prior-year period. The 
increase in net interest income was driven by higher average deposit balances. 
 
Services operating expenses of $3.0 billion increased 10%, primarily driven by higher compensation, including 
higher severance, performance-related and other compensation, transactional expenses and other volume and 
revenue-related expenses. 
 
Services provision for credit losses was a benefit of $2 6 million, reflecting a net reserve release of $ 29 million, 
driven by refinements to certain loss assumptions, and $ 3 million of net credit losses. The provision in the prior -
year period was $61 million, reflecting a net reserve build of $50 million, driven by transfer risk, and $11 million of 
net credit losses.
