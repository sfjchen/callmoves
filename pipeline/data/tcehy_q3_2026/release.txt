BUSINESS REVIEW AND OUTLOOK
Our recent significant achievements across models and applications have accelerated 
Tencent ’s evolution for the new AI era. Hy4 has achieved China state-of-the-art 
AI model status, and Hy4 flash brings down inference costs to deliver cost performance 
for real-world use cases. We believe WorkBuddy has grown to become the largest AI 
application in China, measured by total daily large language model token consumption 
by users. Our cloud services revenue growth rate increased, driven by WorkBuddy and as 
we allocated more compute to Model as a Service and GPU rental. Our core businesses 
remained resilient and vibrant as several evergreen games reached lifetime highs in DAU, 
and as Video Accounts increased engagement rapidly, particularly among young users, by 
enriching its content offering.
Below are some highlights from our key products and services for recent months:
h In November, we released the Hy4 model, with significant advances in foundational 
capabilities and deep model-product co-design, unlocking a broad range of real-world 
productivity use cases. Hy4 ranked first 1 among Chinese models at launch by Agent 
Arena, demonstrating superior real-world capabilities across signals such as task 
success, user satisfaction, and steerability.
h WorkBuddy became the most used AI product in China, measured by large language 
model token consumption, with over 10 million DAU collectively consuming over 100 
trillion tokens per day, and rapidly increasing enterprise adoption.

– Revenues from VAS increased by 3% year-on-year to RMB98.3 billion for the third

automates ad insertion and optimises placement timing and duration. Within the Weixin 
ecosystem, we deepened the integration of closed-loop marketing capabilities, enabling 
Mini Dramas, Mini Shops and other Weixin-integrated services to advertise more 
effectively within Weixin and other Tencent-managed ad properties. Major industry 
categories, notably consumer goods and local services, increased marketing spending 
on our platforms during the quarter.

Selling and marketing expenses . Selling and marketing expenses increased by 6% 
year-on-year to RMB12.1 billion in the third quarter of 2026, mainly due to promotional 
efforts to drive greater adoption of our AI-native products including WorkBuddy.
General and administrative expenses . General and administrative expenses were RMB44.1 
billion for the third quarter of 2026, up 29% year-on-year, primarily driven by higher 
R&D spending to improve our Hy model family, advance Weixin AI initiatives and 
develop AI capabilities across our products and services. Excluding R&D, our general 
and administrative expenses were RMB11.7 billion, up 2% year-on-year, as we controlled 
non-AI related expenses.
Operating profit. Operating profit decreased by 7% year-on-year to RMB59.1 billion in the 
third quarter of 2026. Non-IFRS operating profit decreased by 6% year-on-year to RMB68.1 
billion. Non-IFRS operating profit, excluding new AI products ’3 contributions, increased by 
8% year-on-year to RMB83.3 billion.
Interest income . Interest income decreased by 6% year-on-year to RMB4.0 billion for the 
third quarter of 2026 due to lower average interest yield.
Finance costs . Finance costs were RMB3.5 billion for the third quarter of 2026, compared 
with RMB3.8 billion in the same period last year, reflecting favourable foreign exchange 
movements, partially offset by higher interest expenses due to increased debt.
Share of profit/(loss) of associates and joint ventures, net . Our share of profits of associates 
and joint ventures was RMB4.5 billion for the third quarter of 2026, compared with share 
of profits of RMB7.8 billion in the same period last year. On a non-IFRS basis, our share of 
profits of associates and joint ventures was RMB8.8 billion, compared with share of profits 
of RMB10.3 billion in the same period last year, primarily due to lower profits from certain 
associates in the China internet industry, factoring in market consensus estimates and true-up 
adjustments.
Income tax expense . Income tax expense decreased by 7% year-on-year to RMB9.1 billion 
for the third quarter of 2026, mainly due to lower tax expenses associated with a certain 
overseas subsidiary. Excluding this impact, our income tax expense would have increased 
year-on-year.
Profit attributable to equity holders of the Company . Profit attributable to equity holders 
of the Company increased by 0.8% year-on-year to RMB63.6 billion for the third quarter 
of 2026. Non-IFRS profit attributable to equity holders of the Company decreased by 6% 
year-on-year to RMB66.5 billion.

Non-IFRS Financial Measures
To supplement the consolidated results of the Group prepared in accordance with IFRS, 
certain additional non-IFRS financial measures (in terms of operating profit, operating 
margin, profit for the period, profit attributable to equity holders of the Company, basic EPS 
and diluted EPS) have been presented in this announcement. These unaudited non-IFRS 
financial measures should be considered in addition to, not as a substitute for, measures of 
the Group ’s financial performance prepared in accordance with IFRS. In addition, these 
non-IFRS financial measures may be defined differently from similar terms used by other 
companies.
The Company ’s management believes that the non-IFRS financial measures provide 
investors with useful supplementary information to assess the performance of the Group ’s 
core operations by excluding certain non-cash items and certain impact of investment-related 
items. In addition, non-IFRS adjustments include relevant non-IFRS adjustments for 
the Group ’s major associates based on available published financials of the relevant 
major associates, or estimates made by the Company ’s management based on available 
information, certain expectations, assumptions and premises.

equity interest of one of the Group ’s existing investee companies accounted for as FVPL, which is a

RMB14.0 billion, which comprised cash consideration of approximately RMB8.0 billion, the fair value of

from the additional ordinary shares issued by Tencent Music as part of the consideration.

were not material.
